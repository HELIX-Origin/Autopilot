## How to Log a Bug
1. Copy template from `.agents/templates/bug.md` to `.agents/bugs/BUG-<id>.md`.
2. Fill in reproduction steps, expected vs actual behavior, and error logs.
3. Add the bug to the Open Bugs table above.
